Installations

Schlosserzählen #3

2023  /  with Eunike Kramer  /  Stadtschloss Weimar

The radio artists Eunike Kramer and David Bilek contribute to the urban outdoor space with their audio installation, offering various perspectives on the palace and its history. Passersby on the streets of Weimar associate different impressions with the Residenzschloss — for some it resembles an unopened black box, others carry personal memories of events that took place there.

Their expressions are accompanied by a composition of sounds from the palace itself. Arriving in the present, the palace also demands a critical stance: must we preserve our symbols of authority? And whose version of the past should be told in the present?

The audio installation could be heard daily in the passage to the courtyard of the city palace.

Future Nostalgia

2021 – 2022  /  with Nadja Kracunovic, Denis Lee, Dean Ruddock

Dear listeners, welcome to Future Nostalgia FM, our «time capsule». This is the home to all tiny utopias. We offer you possible scenarios and alternative futures. Our content goes far away from what is known. Its fictional nature allows one's imagination to simply float. Before the radio starts, I would like to remind you to never stop speculating on your future.

Future Nostalgia FM is an experimental, imaginary radio station that deals with the constant uncertainty of our times, combining speculative storytelling, sounds, and audio that overlap eras — from the distant past to the possible future. Its narrative wavers between speculations, aspirations, alternative versions, and perspectives of reality.

The project was conceptualized as an imaginary radio, and it has been played through already existing radio stations such as RadioAparat in Belgrade, generations.fm within the Kultursymposium Weimar, radio CORAX in Halle, bauhaus.fm, and Radio Lotte in Weimar, and the radio program of the Hörspielsommer in Leipzig 2021. In the galleries and exhibition spaces, it functions as a sound installation.
